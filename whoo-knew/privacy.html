WHOO KNEW?

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy explains what information the Whoo Knew? app ("the app", "we") uses, why, and the choices you have. We keep it simple: the app has no accounts, works offline, and does not send your reading history anywhere.

Information stored only on your device

The following stays on your phone and is never uploaded to us or anyone else:

  • Facts you have read, your favorites and your daily ticket history and streak
  • The topics you picked and your notification time
  • Your language choice

Deleting the app removes all of this data from your device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a daily fact reminder on your device at the time you choose. Reminders are created by the app itself; we do not run a notification server and do not receive any information when a reminder is shown or tapped. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system and app version
  • How you interact with ads (views and taps)

On iOS, the app asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. If you do not agree, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your phone's settings. To learn how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Links to other websites

Facts include a link to a source, such as a Wikipedia article. Tapping it opens that site in your browser, and that site's own privacy policy applies.

What we don't do

  • We don't have user accounts and don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
