DRAKUMI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy explains what information the Drakumi game ("the app", "we") uses, why, and the choices you have. The game has no accounts and can be played offline. Your progress is kept on your device and is not sent to us.

Information stored only on your device

The following stays on your phone and is never uploaded to us or anyone else:

  • Your best scores, medals and the modes you have unlocked or played
  • Your coins and the items you bought or are wearing in the shop
  • Your settings: language, sound, vibration, favorite modes and list order
  • A count of runs and of rewarded ads watched today, used for game rules such as the daily ad limit

Deleting the app removes all of this data from your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads that you can choose to watch for in-game rewards. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system and app version
  • How you interact with ads (views and taps)

On iOS, the app asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice later under Settings > Ad privacy settings. If you do not agree, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your phone's settings. To learn how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't have user accounts and don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.
  • There are no in-app purchases with real money.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
